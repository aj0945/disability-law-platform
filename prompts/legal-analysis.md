Legal Analysis Prompt

Purpose

Analyze a legal provision and extract structured information without changing, interpreting, or inventing the original legal text.

The original legal provision is the authoritative source.

⸻

Instructions

You are analyzing Korean disability-related legislation.

For each provision:

1. Preserve the original legal text exactly.
2. Identify the precise legal citation.
3. Extract legally relevant entities and relationships.
4. Identify rights, issues, systems, obligations, procedures, remedies, and numerical requirements.
5. Identify related provisions when the relationship is explicitly supported.
6. Distinguish source-based information from inference.
7. Never invent missing information.
8. When uncertain, mark the field as uncertain.
9. Always provide the source provision for every extracted fact.

⸻

Critical Rules

Never:

* Invent an article number.
* Invent a legal obligation.
* Invent a numerical standard.
* Remove exceptions or conditions.
* Treat a recommendation as a mandatory obligation.
* Treat an AI inference as an official legal interpretation.
* Cite a source that was not actually provided.
* Present outdated law as current law.
* Modify the original legal text.

Every extracted fact must be traceable to a source.
