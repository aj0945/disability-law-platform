
Disability Law Platform

AI-powered knowledge platform for disability rights law and policy.

Vision

Disability Law Platform is an AI-assisted research platform designed to help disability rights advocates, researchers, and policymakers navigate complex legal systems.

Rather than searching by law name, users can search by real-world issues such as:

* Special transportation services
* Supported housing
* Personal assistance services
* Reasonable accommodation
* Boarding bridges
* Assistive technology

The platform automatically connects:

* Laws
* Enforcement Decrees
* Enforcement Rules
* Administrative Rules
* Court Decisions
* UN CRPD
* General Comments
* User Notes

Goals

* Automatically synchronize Korean disability-related laws from the Korean Law Information Center.
* Detect legal amendments.
* Analyze each article using AI.
* Build a searchable knowledge graph for disability rights.
* Support legislative research and advocacy.

Status

🚧 Planning Phase

This repository currently contains the project design documents.

Implementation will begin after the system architecture and data model are finalized.

## Information Access Model

This project distinguishes between public legal information and internal advocacy knowledge.

### Public Layer

Includes publicly available information:

- Laws
- Enforcement Decrees
- Enforcement Rules
- International conventions
- General Comments
- Public court decisions
- Public policy documents

### Restricted Layer

Includes organization-specific knowledge:

- Advocacy strategies
- Internal legal analysis
- Negotiation materials
- Campaign planning documents
- Unpublished policy proposals
- Field experience notes

Access to restricted information must be controlled separately.

The platform is designed to preserve collective knowledge while protecting strategic information.
