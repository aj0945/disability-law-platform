# Requirements

## 1. Project Goal

### Purpose

The Disability Law Platform is an AI-assisted knowledge platform designed to support disability rights advocacy, legal research, and policy development.

The platform connects laws, enforcement decrees, enforcement rules, international human rights standards, court decisions, policy documents, and accumulated advocacy knowledge.

The goal is not only to search legal information, but to help users understand rights, identify legal grounds, and develop advocacy strategies.

---

## 2. Users

### Primary User: Disability Rights Advocates

Users who engage in disability rights movements, policy advocacy, and legal campaigns.

Needs:

- Find legal grounds quickly
- Understand obligations and rights in laws
- Prepare policy proposals and negotiations
- Transfer accumulated knowledge to new activists

---

### Secondary User: Researchers and Policy Practitioners

Users who study disability policy or develop legislation.

Needs:

- Compare domestic and international legal systems
- Analyze legislative history
- Find supporting evidence

---

### Administrator

Users who manage the platform.

Needs:

- Manage user permissions
- Review uploaded information
- Maintain data quality

---

## 3. Core Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Searching by Issue

Users search real-world problems rather than only legal names.

Example:

Input:
"Wheelchair users cannot access intercity buses"

System provides:

- Relevant rights
- Related legal provisions
- Enforcement regulations
- International standards
- Relevant cases
- Advocacy materials

---

### Use Case 2: Researching a Legal System

Example:

Input:
"Special Transportation Services"

System provides:

- Related laws
- Enforcement decrees
- Enforcement rules
- Administrative guidelines
- Amendment history
- Related documents

---

### Use Case 3: Legislative Advocacy

Example:

Input:
"Need to establish special transportation operation standards"

System provides:

- Current legal framework
- Legal limitations
- International examples
- Supporting evidence
- Previous advocacy materials

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Legal Information Management

The system shall:

- Collect disability-related laws automatically
- Connect laws, enforcement decrees, and enforcement rules
- Maintain amendment history
- Provide original sources

---

### 4.2 Knowledge Organization

The system shall organize information by:

- Rights
- Issues
- Systems
- Legal obligations
- Responsible organizations
- Target groups

---

### 4.3 AI Analysis

The AI shall extract:

- Subject of obligation
- Target group
- Rights involved
- Legal obligations
- Prohibited actions
- Numbers and deadlines
- Procedures
- Enforcement mechanisms

The AI shall not make independent legal conclusions without source references.

---

## 5. Information Access Control

The system shall separate:

### Public Information

- Laws
- Regulations
- International conventions
- Public decisions
- Public reports

### Organization Information

- Internal legal analysis
- Advocacy strategies
- Negotiation materials
- Unpublished proposals

### Personal Information

- Individual research notes
- Draft ideas
- Private annotations

Access shall be controlled according to information sensitivity.

---

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

The system should:

- Provide clear source citations
- Maintain version history
- Record contributors
- Support continuous updates
- Allow knowledge transfer between activists

---

## 7. Future Expansion

Potential future functions:

- International legal comparison
- Case law database
- Advocacy document archive
- AI-assisted legislative drafting
- Training materials generation
